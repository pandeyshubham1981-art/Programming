Welcome to Amritsari Zaika

Nestled in the heart of Amritsar, Amritsari Zaika brings you the real taste of Punjab. From tandoor-fresh kulchas to slow-cooked dal makhani, every dish is prepared with pure desi ghee, hand-ground spices and recipes passed down through three generations.

About Us & Our Services

We are a family-run vegetarian and non-vegetarian restaurant known for warm hospitality and generous portions. Whether it is a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a big celebration, we make every meal memorable.

Our Services:
  • Family dine-in & private party hall
  • Takeaway and home delivery (within 8 km)
  • Outdoor catering for weddings & events
  • Online table booking (below)
  • Hygienic kitchen, FSSAI certified
